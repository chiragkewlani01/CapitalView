CapitalView

Financial Intelligence & Cash-Flow Management Platform

CapitalView is a financial intelligence platform that helps businesses monitor cash flow, track receivables and payables, forecast future liquidity, and identify potential financial risks from a single dashboard.

Problem

Businesses often manage financial data across spreadsheets, accounting software, bank statements, and separate reports. This makes it difficult to get a clear picture of:

. Current cash position

. Expected income and collections

. Upcoming payments and expenses

. Future cash availability

. Potential cash-flow risks

CapitalView brings this information together into one centralized financial intelligence platform.

Key Features

💰 Cash Management

Monitor current cash balance and liquidity

Track inflows and outflows

Set minimum cash thresholds

View bank and cash account balances

📊 Cash-Flow Analysis

Analyze historical cash flow

Track revenue, expenses, payroll, taxes, and loan payments

Identify spending and cash-flow trends

📥 Accounts Receivable

Track outstanding and overdue invoices

Monitor customer payment timelines

Estimate expected collections

Analyze total receivables

📤 Accounts Payable

Track supplier invoices and obligations

Monitor upcoming and overdue payments

Estimate future cash requirements

🔮 Cash-Flow Forecasting

Forecast future cash positions using:

Projected Cash = Opening Cash + Expected Inflows - Expected Outflows

⚠️ Financial Risk Monitoring

Identify potential liquidity problems such as:

Low cash reserves

Delayed customer payments

High upcoming obligations

Increasing expenses

Potential cash shortages

Dashboard

The CapitalView dashboard provides a centralized view of key financial metrics, trends, forecasts, and alerts to help management make faster and better-informed decisions.

Tech Stack

Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript

Backend: FastAPI

Database: PostgreSQL (Neon)

AI: OpenAI API

Project Goal

The goal of CapitalView is to transform raw financial data into actionable insights so businesses can understand where their money is, where it is going, and what their future cash position may look like.
